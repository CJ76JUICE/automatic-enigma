Our Story

Chalet 222® is a small creative studio born from a love of handcrafted beauty and intentional design. Every piece we create carries a story — shaped by skilled hands, inspired by nature, and finished with care.

What We Create

Our collections span three distinct crafts: original home décor under the 222 Dêcor line, one-of-a-kind paintings by Ashley, and the signature Beautifully Broken® glass and metal art created by Kim. Each item is designed and made in small batches, ensuring that what you bring home is truly unique.

Paintings by Ashley — Made For You

Every painting by Ashley is a truly personal creation. No two are alike, and none are made until you order one. Ashley works directly with each client to understand their vision — the colors that move them, the moments they want to capture, the feeling they want a piece to carry into their home.

From that conversation, Ashley begins a process that is entirely hand-painted, start to finish. No prints, no reproductions. Your painting is conceived, sketched, and brought to life specifically for you, reflecting your story in every brushstroke.

Special orders are welcomed and encouraged. Whether you have a clear concept or just a feeling you can't quite put into words, Ashley will guide you through the process and craft something that feels unmistakably yours. Reach out to start a conversation.

Beautifully Broken® — Glass & Metal

The Beautifully Broken® name reflects artist Kim's belief that there is profound beauty in imperfection. From the fractured lines of repurposed glass to the expressive energy of metal work, each piece Kim creates celebrates the art of transforming the unexpected into something extraordinary.

Our Philosophy

We create slowly and intentionally. No mass production — every item leaves our studio as a one-of-a-kind work of art. When you purchase from Chalet 222®, you're not just buying a product; you're taking home a piece of someone's creative journey.
