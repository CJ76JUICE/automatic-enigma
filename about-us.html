Our Story

Chalet 42® is a small creative studio born from a love of handcrafted beauty and intentional design. Every piece we create carries a story — shaped by skilled hands, inspired by nature, and finished with care.

What We Create

Our collections span four distinct crafts: original home décor under the Décor line, one-of-a-kind paintings by Ashley, the signature Mixed glass and metal art created by Kim, and fine art prints by graphic designer Carrie. Each item is designed and made with care, ensuring that what you bring home is truly unique.

Paintings — Made For You

Every painting by Ashley is a truly personal creation. No two are alike, and none are made until you order one. Ashley works directly with each client to understand their vision — the colors that move them, the moments they want to capture, the feeling they want a piece to carry into their home.

From that conversation, Ashley begins a process that is entirely hand-painted, start to finish. No prints, no reproductions. Your painting is conceived, sketched, and brought to life specifically for you, reflecting your story in every brushstroke.

Special orders are welcomed and encouraged. Whether you have a clear concept or just a feeling you can't quite put into words, Ashley will guide you through the process and craft something that feels unmistakably yours. Reach out to start a conversation.

Mixed Media — Glass & Metal

The Mixed name reflects artist Kim's belief that there is profound beauty in imperfection. From the fractured lines of repurposed glass to the expressive energy of metal work, each piece Kim creates celebrates the art of transforming the unexpected into something extraordinary.

Print — Carrie

Carrie is a graphic designer with a passion for the amalgamation of life — drawing inspiration from the places, textures, and living things that surround us. Her prints blend natural beauty with contemporary design, creating pieces that feel at once familiar and singular.

Each print is a reflection of that philosophy: carefully composed, professionally produced, and designed to bring lasting character to any space.

Looking for something personalized? Carrie welcomes custom print requests. Reach out to start a conversation.

Our Philosophy

We create slowly and intentionally. No mass production — every item leaves our studio as a one-of-a-kind work of art. When you purchase from Chalet 42®, you're not just buying a product; you're taking home a piece of someone's creative journey.
